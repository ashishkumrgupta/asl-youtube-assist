## Terms (plain language)

1. Clips are **citation-length** (about 2–3 seconds), front-facing torso + face, for lexical assist — not discourse interpretation.
2. The product will label clips as **ASL assist / partial**, with volunteer source honesty and **not** interpreter / gold / accuracy claims.
3. Credits appear as you prefer (name, handle, or anonymous).
4. You may request removal of future builds; already-installed versions may retain prior packs until users update.
5. Dialect / regional variation may mean a clip is imperfect for some viewers — we disclose limited scope.
6. No payment is offered in the current $0 CapEx phase unless the owner later funds contributors.
